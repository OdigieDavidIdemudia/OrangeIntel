Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my intelligence data stored?

All of your data—from ingested feeds to completed advisory reports—is stored in a local, AES-256 encrypted SQLite database. We do not sync your active investigations to any cloud provider.

How does the AI integration work?

When you initiate a DOCX export, the raw JSON of your report is sent directly to an advanced AI model for strict formatting, typo correction, and tone adjustment. This happens silently and returns a validated payload.

Can I add custom feeds?

Yes. The platform natively supports ingestion from any standard RSS feed, alongside deep API integrations with CISA KEV and AlienVault OTX.

What is required for the automated email alerts?

To enable automated advisory emails for high-severity threats, simply input your own API keys for your preferred Email provider in the Settings dashboard. TealHunt will handle the formatting and attachment delivery.
